why the bird?

Why Jatayu.

Jatayu is the watchful one of the Ramayana, the great bird who saw Sita carried off and rose against Ravana, old and outmatched as he was. Defeated, he spent his last breath on the one thing that mattered: the direction Ravana had flown. One true word, and the rescue had its beginning.

Sight is worth little without the word that follows it.

An intelligence worth building works the same way. It watches closely, understands what it sees, and returns the one word that lets a person act, at the moment it counts.

साक्षीwitness
दिशाdirection
धर्मdharma
house rules

Three lines from one old story.

CHIT · 01

Watch with care.

Notice clearly before acting. A guardian's first job is attention, not announcement.

CHIT · 02

Say what is true.

An honest answer, an honest limit, an honest error. The real word, not the convenient one.

CHIT · 03

Make the last word count.

One precise word can set a rescue in motion. Every reply is built like it's that one.
