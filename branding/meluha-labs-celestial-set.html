meluha — the celestial set

Ten directions in the register you liked in mihir’s Graha sun — geometric, celestial, dimensional, nothing illustrative. No boats, no sails. Each one still earns its India: the lunar mansions, the Saptarṣi, the Dholavira sign, the 1:2:4 brick, the eclipse of Rahu, the bindu. Ink structure · kiln-gradient life · one indigo decision — and the Graha orbit trick (passing behind, then in front) wherever it adds depth.

★ shortlist

01Chandra-mārga

The crescent on its ring road, one indigo mansion ahead of it. India’s calendar IS the moon walking its 27 houses — this is Graha’s exact sibling: he holds the sun, we ride the moon.

★ shortlist

02Dholavira chakra

The spoked-wheel sign from the Dholavira signboard — the oldest public inscription in the subcontinent — set spinning inside a kiln-gradient orbit. The Indus sign that became the chakra on the flag: five thousand years of one mark, ours at the start of it.

★ shortlist

03Bindu — the dotted circle

⊙ is one of the commonest signs in the Indus script — and the bindu is where all Indian geometry begins: the point from which everything is drawn. The most reduced mark possible that is still both “Meluha” and “India”.

04Saptarṣi

The Seven Sages — India’s name for the Great Bear, the dial every northern navigator read — with the pole star burning warm. Constellation as wordless brand: seven indigo decisions pointing at one fixed light.

05Vesica — the two ports

Lothal and Akkad: two worlds, and the lens where they overlap glowing kiln-warm. Trade, translation, collaboration — the entire Meluha story told in two circles. Flawless at any size; the most “systems” mark here.

06Iṣṭakā — 1 : 2 : 4

Every brick from Harappa to Lothal keeps one ratio — 1:2:4 — the first industrial standard on earth. Three bricks in true proportion, the unit brick fired warm, an orbit of ambition around the stack. “We standardize, then we build.”

07Śaṅkha — the shell spiral

Meluha ran the world’s first shell workshops — conch bangles cut at Nageshwar travelled a thousand miles. A kiln spiral opening outward inside the horizon ring: compounding, growth, the long game.

08Rāhu — the eclipse

India explained eclipses with a story and then computed them with mathematics — both five centuries before anyone else wrote the method down. The bitten sun on its node-ring: curiosity exactly where the light gets strange.

09Darpaṇa — sun on the water

Mihir keeps the sun; Meluha keeps its reflection — the sun as the river receives it, broken into usable light. The cleanest possible “sibling labs” story, and a beautiful loading animation waiting to happen.

10The m, in orbit

If you want a pure monogram: a kiln-gradient lowercase m built from two arches — the corbelled gateways of Harappa — with the Graha ring threading it. Zero archaeology required of the viewer; the orbit does the talking.

Where I’d look first: 01 Chandra-mārga is the truest sibling to the mihir Graha sun — same dimensional ring, opposite luminary, and the nakshatra story underneath; the pair reads as “the sun lab and the moon lab”. 02 Dholavira chakra is the boldest India claim in the whole exploration — the actual oldest sign, ancestor of the chakra on the flag. 03 Bindu is the minimalist’s pick: an authentic Indus glyph that is also the first mark of Indian geometry. If none of these land either, the honest next move is a half-hour together picking the one *story* you want the company to tell — vessel, sign, sky, or standard — and I’ll take that single story to finished-mark depth instead of breadth.
