“Ugra” (उग्र) — fierce, formidable; the intense aspect of Rudra-Shiva, one of his eight forms, and the root of Ugra-Narasimha, the protector who tears through the impossible. Three marks, one rule: a quiet geometric symbol + a confident lowercase wordmark — fierceness, drawn with restraint, on the palette the product already lives in.

Trinetra — the fierce eye

Ugra is the fierce aspect of Rudra, and the third eye is where that fierceness lives — the eye that opens and burns through. Drawn vertical (a trinetra, never a common eye), its iris the dawn gradient, three sparks rising as it opens. For a labs brand it’s the perfect double-read: fierce intelligence that sees. The most distinctive of the three at icon scale.

Jwala — the harnessed flame

Ugra as agni — primal fire — but held inside a precise ink ring: raw power under exact control, which is what a lab is. The flame cools from saffron at its base to indigo at the tip, the full dawn ramp folded into a single rising figure. Warm, alive, unmistakably ours — and a natural “thinking” mark while a turn is working.

Trishula — the three-pronged strike

Shiva’s trishula — the weapon of the fierce one — redrawn as clean ascending geometry: three dawn-gradient prongs over an ink shaft, bound at a single node. Three points, one strike — decisive, unmistakably Shaiva, unmistakably Ugra. The most “force” of the three, and the one that scales best as a flat wordmark companion.

Recommendation: 01 Trinetra as the primary brand mark — it carries the whole idea in one figure (fierce sight, the dawn iris, sparks rising) and is the strongest at 16–48 px, so it doubles as the app icon. 02 Jwala as the warm secondary / loading mark (the flame reads beautifully animated while the agent is “looking that up”). 03 Trishula for power moments — splash, stationery, the landing hero. Wordmark in Clash Display SemiBold, all-lowercase “ugra” with “labs” in regular — fierce name, restrained voice. The Devanagari उग्र sits as a secondary line on stationery and the footer, never inside the mark.
