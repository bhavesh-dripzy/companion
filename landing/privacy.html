This website

This notice covers jatayulabs.com and its interactive product demonstration. The company website does not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or marketing cookies. Fonts and site assets are hosted with the site.

Demo requests

The Get demo contact link opens your email application. A request is sent only when you send the email. The website does not collect or store an email address through a signup form.

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, company, and enquiry. We use it to respond and discuss a product demonstration or early access. Our email provider processes those messages as part of delivering email.

The interactive demonstration

All assets, risks, records, and evidence in the demonstration are fictional. The demonstration does not connect to production systems or perform security testing. Demo notes are held in memory in your current browser tab and are not submitted to a server.

Hosting and technical information

The site is delivered through Amazon Web Services. Hosting providers necessarily process technical information, including network addresses and request details, to deliver and secure the website. We do not use this information for advertising or visitor profiling.

Your choices

Do not include passwords, access tokens, or sensitive security evidence in a demo request. You can ask us about the information you have provided, request its correction or deletion, or ask us not to contact you further.

Contact

Email founders@jatayulabs.com with privacy questions or requests.
