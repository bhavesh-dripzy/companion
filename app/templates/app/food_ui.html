📦 How to connect Zomato MCP

  1. Click Get OAuth URL above — this tries mcp-remote and captures the Zomato authorization link.
  2. Open the URL in your browser and approve Zomato access.
  3. After authorization, tokens are saved to ~/.mcp-auth/ on your local machine.
  4. On your machine: ls ~/.mcp-auth/ and cat ~/.mcp-auth/<file>
  5. Paste each filename and its content into the form below.
  6. Click Upload All to AWS — tokens stored in Secrets Manager.
  7. Click Test Connection — Lambda restores tokens and connects to Zomato.
