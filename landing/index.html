Why Jatayu.

Jatayu is the watchful one of the Ramayana, the great bird who saw Sita carried off and rose against Ravana, old and outmatched as he was. Defeated, he spent his last breath on the one thing that mattered, the direction Ravana had flown. One true word, and the rescue had its beginning.

Sight is worth little without the word that follows it.

An intelligence worth building works the same way. It watches closely, it understands what it sees, and it returns the one word that lets a person act, at the moment it counts. That is the standard we hold our models to.

how we work

Three lines from one old story.

01

Watch with care.

Perception is not a feature we bolt on at the end. We build to notice clearly, the way a guardian keeps watch over what matters.

02

Say what is true.

An honest answer, an honest limit, an honest error. The word we hand back is the real one, not the convenient one.

03

Make the last word count.

When everything is on the line, one precise word can change all that follows. We treat every signal like the one that sets the search in motion.
